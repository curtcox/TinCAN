Console

A full TinCAN node. It transmits, acknowledges, arbitrates and confines errors. Open it on two devices in the same room, set them to the same band and timing profile, and they form a bus. Open it on one device and it will transmit into an empty room, go error-passive, and tell you nobody is listening — which is exactly what §8.3 says should happen.
